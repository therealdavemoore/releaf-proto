First time?

Here's how it works

01

Browse the humidor

Walk in and take your time. Our staff will let you look around, and they'll come to you when you're ready — no pressure, no hover.

Tell us your experience level, what you've liked before, or what you're in the mood for. We know these cigars inside and out.

Find a chair, get comfortable. The lounge is open to all customers — no table minimum, no time limit, no attitude.

People come in once and never stop coming back. That's the goal — to be your spot in Portland for the long run.
