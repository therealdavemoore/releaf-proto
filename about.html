A shop built

We opened in 2021 because Portland deserved somewhere to sit down, slow down, and actually enjoy what you’re smoking. A walk-in humidor, a private lounge, and staff who know what they’re talking about.

How it started

A neighborhood shop,
built for the long game.

Releaf opened in 2021 in Southeast Portland. Our founder Marcus Webb had spent fifteen years running front-of-house at some of the city’s best restaurants — and in all that time, he kept noticing the same gap: Portland had great bars, great coffee, great food. It didn’t have a great cigar shop.

So he built one. The idea was simple: a shop that takes the product seriously without taking itself too seriously. A walk-in humidor with staff who actually know what’s in it. A lounge where you can sit as long as you want. No membership, no attitude, no upsell.

The name came easily. Releaf — a nod to the leaf at the center of everything we do, and to the reason most people reach for a cigar in the first place. To exhale. To be somewhere other than wherever you were.

We’re not here to sell you the most expensive thing in the case. We’re here to help you find the right one.

Marcus Webb, Founder

What we believe

A few things we don’t
negotiate on.

Know the product.

We taste every cigar we stock. If we can’t speak to it — the blend, the origin, the right occasion — we don’t carry it. Curation is part of the job.

No pressure, ever.

You’re spending your time in our shop. The least we can do is make it comfortable. Our staff will come to you when you’re ready — not a moment before.

The lounge is for everyone.

Buy a cigar, take a seat. No table minimum, no time limit, no membership card. The lounge is open to every customer, every hour we’re open.

Curation over volume.

We don’t stock 2,000 cigars. We stock the ones worth carrying. Every purchase comes with a staff recommendation if you want one.

Experience level doesn’t matter.

Whether it’s your first cigar or your thousandth, you get the same conversation. We’d rather talk cigars than impress you with how much we know about them.

Portland deserves this.

This city has given us more than we expected. Our goal is to be a shop worth having — one that’s still here in twenty years, still doing exactly this.

The humidor & lounge

Walk in and take
your time.

Take a left when you come through the door and you’re in the humidor — hundreds of cigars, organized by country and strength, with handwritten staff notes on most of what we carry. Take as long as you need. Nobody is going to rush you.

When you’re ready, find a seat in the lounge. We designed it to feel like the best room in a very good house: comfortable chairs, good light, no hurry. It’s open every hour we’re open, and there’s no clock on how long you stay.

We’re happy to check inventory, answer questions, or hold something for you.
