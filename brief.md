- Ships nationwide across the US

- Phone: (971) 202-1196

## Current Site Audit (releafcigars.com on Shopify)

### Catalog
- **1,344 total SKUs** across 56+ pages
- 726 in stock / 618 out of stock
- Price range: $7.20–$28 per cigar; bundles up to $200
- Brands include: 1502, 1916 Tabacalera Eiroa, 300 Hands, 601 La Bomba, A/Ramos Beethov, and 50+ more
- Formats: singles, 5-packs, bundles, gift sets, travel humidors

### Navigation (current — flat, 5 links)
- Home / My Account / About / Brands / Contact

### E-Commerce Features (current)
- Automatic 10% discount at $99 spend
- Free shipping threshold at $49
- Quick-add to cart
- Cart progress banner ("$X away from free shipping")
- Multiple payment methods (Amex, Visa, MC, Discover, Diners, JCB)

### Trust Signals (current)
- Age verification notice (21+)
- Payment method logos
- No customer reviews or testimonials on homepage
- No guarantee language

### What's Working
- Discount and shipping incentives are clearly surfaced
- Product photography is consistent and clean
- Quick-shop functionality

### What's Broken or Missing
- Homepage shows ~6 products — massively undersells a 1,344 SKU catalog
- No filtering by wrapper, strength, origin, or format
- The walk-in humidor and private lounge are completely invisible on the homepage
- Brand story is essentially blank (About page = 4 sentences of operational info)
- No reviews or social proof
- Non-functional country selector appearing redundantly throughout the page
- No educational content or editorial layer

---

## Inspiration: Cigars Direct (cigarsdirect.com)

### What to Learn From
- **Mega-menu** with filtering by brand, wrapper, strength, shape, origin, and price
- **Multiple discovery paths** to the same product (browsing by wrapper OR strength OR brand all converge)
- **"Cigars of the Year"** and editorial content sections — positions them as experts
- **Samplers and 5-packs** as a primary, first-class product category
- **Shipping threshold progress bar** — persistent psychological nudge
- **Customer testimonials** section
- **Educational blog** ("Fresh Cigar News") — beginner guides, pairings, etc.
- **Order tracking integration**

---

## Key Opportunities for the Redesign

1. **Lead with the story** — Portland shop, walk-in humidor, private lounge. That's an experience, not just a store.
2. **Navigation overhaul** — Mega-menu or structured category nav by brand, wrapper, strength, format, and price
3. **The Lounge as a feature** — dedicated section/page with photos, booking, events
4. **Discovery and filtering** — wrapper type, strength, origin, format filters for the full catalog
5. **Social proof layer** — reviews, testimonials, guarantee language
6. **Samplers & gift bundles** — entry products for new customers and gift buyers
7. **Editorial/content layer** — blog, staff picks, pairings — builds SEO and expertise positioning
8. **Design elevation** — dark/rich palette, strong typography, atmospheric photography
