Reviews (48)

The F-Bomb is 601's most powerful expression — a full-bodied Nicaraguan puro wrapped in an inky San Andrés Maduro. From the first cut to the final third, it delivers exactly what the name promises: an unapologetic, high-strength smoke with remarkable complexity.

The draw is effortless. You get dark cocoa and earth upfront, with a natural sweetness from the Mexican Maduro wrapper that softens the punch without dumbing it down. Mid-palate transitions to leather and roasted coffee. The finish is long, bold, and memorable.

This is a cigar for experienced smokers who know what they want. Pair it with a peaty Islay Scotch or a good cold brew.
